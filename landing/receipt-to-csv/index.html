Frequently asked questions

What columns does the receipt CSV contain?

Each row is one receipt, with eight columns: Date, Merchant, Total, VAT, VAT Rate, Category, Notes and Receipt ID. You can open the file in Excel or import it into Google Sheets or any other spreadsheet app.

Is the data extracted automatically?

The date, merchant and amounts are read from the photo automatically, but not always perfectly. You can review each receipt and correct anything that looks wrong before you export.

Can I export only some of my receipts?

Yes. You choose which receipts to include and can filter them by date: the last 7 days, the last 30 days, this month, last month or a custom date range.

Does Spendify connect to my bank account?

No. Spendify does not connect to banks or financial institutions and does not sync transactions. You upload a receipt, review the data and choose what to export.
