What Ends Up in YNAB

Every receipt becomes one transaction with:

  • ✓ The receipt date
  • ✓ The amount, as an outflow
  • ✓ The merchant as the payee
  • ✓ The memo “Export Spendify”
  • ✓ Status: cleared and approved

No category is sent. You assign categories in YNAB, as you already do.

Your YNAB Categories Stay Intact

Spendify never overwrites categories in YNAB. Transactions arrive without one, so your budget, your rules and your category habits stay in YNAB.

Categories you set inside Spendify are for your own organisation only. They are never sent to YNAB.

Set It Up Once

You connect YNAB with a Personal Access Token, which you create in YNAB under Account Settings → Developer Settings. Then you enter your Budget ID (or use “last-used”) and the Account ID that receipts should go to.

Spendify sends each transaction from your browser directly to YNAB, using your token, which is stored in your browser.

No Double Entries From Spendify

Spendify remembers which receipts you have already sent and leaves them out of the next YNAB export. You can also pick receipts one by one, or filter by date.

One limit to know about: Spendify does not read your existing YNAB transactions, so it cannot tell if a purchase was already imported from your bank.

Frequently asked questions

Which details are sent to YNAB?

Each receipt becomes one transaction with the date, the amount as an outflow, the payee name and the memo “Export Spendify”. It is created in the account you chose, marked as cleared and approved.

Will Spendify change my YNAB categories?

No. Transactions are created without a category, so you keep assigning categories in YNAB the way you always do. Categories you set inside Spendify stay in Spendify and are never sent to YNAB.

Can I send only some receipts to YNAB?

Yes. You pick which receipts to send and can filter them by date. Receipts you have already sent are left out of the next YNAB export, so you do not send the same one twice.

Does it check for transactions already imported from my bank?

No. Spendify does not read your existing YNAB transactions, so it cannot tell whether a purchase was already imported from your bank. Check for a duplicate in YNAB if you use both.

Do I need YNAB to use Spendify?

No. You can also export your receipts as a CSV file for Excel or Google Sheets. See how receipt photos become a spreadsheet.

New accounts get a 14-day free trial, with no credit card required. After the trial, the subscription is €4.99 per month.
