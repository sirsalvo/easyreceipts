    YNAB Integration

    Connect YNAB in 3 Steps

    Export your receipts directly to YNAB. Keep full control of your categories.

    Your Categories Stay Intact

    Spendify never overwrites categories in YNAB. Transactions are created without a category, giving you full control over categorization directly in YNAB.

    1

    Generate Your Token

    Go to YNAB → Account Settings → Developer Settings and create a new Personal Access Token.

    Open YNAB Developer
    2

    Budget ID

    Use 'last-used' to always export to your active budget, or copy the ID from your budget URL.

    Tip: use 'last-used' for simplicity
    3

    Account ID

    In your YNAB account URL, the Account ID is the last parameter after /accounts/

    E.g.: .../accounts/abc123-def456 → abc123-def456
